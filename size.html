    Which is really bigger?

    As Mercator draws them

      The Mercator projection keeps shapes but swells area away from the equator: at 60° every country is drawn four times its size. Most web maps use it. Seven of each day's ten are traps, where the one drawn bigger is really smaller; three are not. Outlines: Natural Earth 1:110m (public domain). Both areas are measured from the outline shown, so they are rounded to two figures; far-off islands (Hawaii, French Guiana) are left out. The 150 countries of 20,000 km² or more. A new ten every day at your midnight; everyone gets the same ten. Nothing leaves your browser. Also: Which Country?, the daily ten from Our World in Data.
