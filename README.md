# Which Country?

**<https://matmat202.github.io/which-country/>**

Two countries, one number. Tap the one that's higher. Where do people live longer, Chile or
Poland? Which emits more CO₂ per person, Bolivia or Colombia? Which has more people, Algeria
or Bosnia and Herzegovina? Ten a day. In the first round the two countries are worlds apart,
and by the last they're neck and neck. After each answer you see both numbers as bars and
where they come from. Everyone gets the same ten, a new set arrives at your midnight, and
your streak and results stay in your browser.

## Where the numbers come from

[Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/) grapher charts, all openly licensed (CC BY
4.0, CC BY 3.0 IGO, or CC0):

| Question | Chart | Year |
|---|---|---|
| Where do people live longer? | [life-expectancy](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/life-expectancy) | 2023 |
| Which emits more CO₂ per person? | [co-emissions-per-capita](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/co-emissions-per-capita) | 2024 |
| Which is richer per person? | [gdp-per-capita-worldbank](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/gdp-per-capita-worldbank) (PPP) | 2025 |
| Which has more people? | [population](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/population) | 2023 |
| Where do more people use the internet? | [share-of-individuals-using-the-internet](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/share-of-individuals-using-the-internet) | 2024 |
| Where do women have more children? | [children-per-woman-un](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/children-per-woman-un) | 2023 |
| Which is more urban? | [share-of-population-urban](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/share-of-population-urban) | 2025 |
| Which has the older population? | [median-age](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/median-age) | 2023 |

The countries are the 160 with a million people or more. Each question uses one year for
every country, the latest one that nine in ten of them have, so a pair is always like for
like. A country with no number that year is just never asked about it. Two countries that
would show the same number are never paired.

Higher only means higher, not better.
